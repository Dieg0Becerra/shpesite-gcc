About SHPE

For over 50 years, SHPE has been the leading organization for Hispanic professionals in STEM, creating opportunities and building bridges to success.

Our Mission

As a chapter at GCC, we understand the unique situation that community college STEM students face compared to traditional university STEM students. That's why we wanna provide a community for all STEM students here at GCC and help them find and get ready for new opportunities
